# Mental models

Thinking clearly · Sample note

A small toolbox for looking at a problem from more than one angle.

## A lens, not a rule

A model is useful when it helps me notice something I would otherwise miss. I want a few ways to think, without turning any one of them into an answer to everything.

## Three useful questions

What am I assuming? What would change my mind? If this goes well immediately, what might happen next? These questions slow down the jump from a convincing story to a decision.

## Keep the exceptions

The interesting part of a model is often where it breaks. I’d keep concrete examples and counterexamples here as this note grows.

> Try a second lens before committing to the first explanation.
