# Attention & control

Philosophy in practice · Sample note

A thought from a book, carried into everyday decisions.

## Separate the two

There is the situation in front of me, and there is the response I can choose. When I mix those together, it becomes difficult to see where I can actually act.

## A working practice

Before a difficult conversation, write down the part that is mine: preparation, clarity, listening. Leave space for the part that belongs to someone else. A future version of this note could include a specific passage and its edition or page number.

> Put the effort where there is a choice.
